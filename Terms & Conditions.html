Terms & Conditions

Everything you need to know for a smooth rental experience with Fenix

Driver Requirements

To ensure safety for all, drivers must meet the following criteria:

  • Minimum age of 21 years
  • Valid, unexpired driver's license
  • Minimum 2 years driving experience
  • Valid identification document

Vehicle Use Policy

Our vehicles must be used responsibly:

  • Personal or business use only
  • No reckless or dangerous driving
  • No illegal activities permitted
  • No smoking inside vehicles

Fuel Policy

Our fuel policy is simple and fair:

  • Vehicles provided with full tank
  • Return with same fuel level
  • Refueling charge if tank not full
  • Use recommended fuel type only

Damage & Liability

Understanding your responsibilities:

  • Walk-around inspection at pickup
  • Report any damage immediately
  • Renter liable for unreported damage
  • Insurance options available

Late Returns

Please be mindful of return times:

  • 1-hour grace period allowed
  • Contact us if running late
  • Hourly charges may apply after grace
  • Full day rate after 4 hours late

Cancellation Policy

Flexible cancellation options:

  • Free cancellation 24+ hours before
  • 50% charge within 24 hours
  • Full charge for no-shows
  • Modifications subject to availability
