About Fenix Car Hire

Your trusted partner for premium vehicle rental services in the Kingdom of Eswatini

Who We Are

Fenix Car Hire is a professional vehicle rental company based in the Kingdom of Eswatini. We provide reliable, safe, and cost-effective mobility solutions to corporate, government, NGO, and private clients across the country.

Operating from our headquarters at Lilanga Complex, Unit B, Sidwashini, we are committed to delivering dependable transport services supported by a well-maintained fleet and customer-focused operations.

Our team of experienced professionals ensures that every rental experience exceeds expectations, whether you need a vehicle for business travel, personal trips, or corporate events.
